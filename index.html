About me

/01

I’m a UX/UI and visual designer, creating websites and digital interfaces with a consistent visual language that extends into marketing materials.

My process spans briefs, wireframes, prototypes, design systems and developer handoff. I integrate AI throughout my design process to explore ideas, develop visuals, build prototypes, and streamline everyday tasks. I have experience collaborating with marketers and developers in international, cross-functional teams.
