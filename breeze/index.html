ENESUARU

Context & goal

/01

A working site, but no bookings.
Goal: make guests book direct.

The owner built the first site herself with AI, and it turned out typical AI slop: a generic template that could belong to any hotel. It had apartment pages, a calendar and a price calculator, yet guests still booked on Booking.com, paying the platform’s commission.

Audit & design decisions

/02

A shorter path to
a booking request.

The audit followed one task: find a free date and see the price. Each screen was checked for what helps a guest decide and what gets in the way. What the old site left unanswered, from seasonal prices to house rules, came from a short brief for the owner. The redesign cut the path to a price and gave every screen a clear order.

Owner brief

Right facts, right place.

The brief revealed that the key facts — prices, booking terms, house rules — were already on the old site, but hidden at the end of the booking page. So the design moved them to where guests decide: the apartment cards and the search results.

UX/UI issues

  1. 01Price at the very end→The price appeared only after four steps on a separate booking page.
  2. 02Reviews lead away→To read reviews, guests left for Booking.com — and booked there.
  3. 03No reason to book direct→Nothing explained why to book here and not on a platform.
  4. 04Overloaded cards→Too much text on apartment cards to compare options at a glance.
  5. 05Generic AI template→A look that could belong to any hotel.
  6. 06No sense of place→The ocean and the light were missing.
  7. 07No visual hierarchy→Offer cards, buttons and headlines competed for attention.

Decisions

Booking flow

Before redesign → 4 steps
Home→Booking page→Dates→Guests→Apartment and price
After redesign → 1 step
Home: dates and guests→Price and free apartments

Page structure

The home page now follows a guest’s decision: what it costs, where it is, why to trust it — and the details before booking. The old site had fewer blocks, but not the ones guests needed to decide: no prices, no reviews, no answers to common questions, and booking lived on a separate page. Those gaps became new blocks, and the booking request now starts right from the search.

Design system & development

/03

One design system:
responsive, consistent, accessible.

One set of rules for typography, spacing and components. Every layout works on desktop and mobile, in three languages, and calculates the total price for the chosen dates. Accessibility was checked with an automated audit.

Result

/04

A clear booking path
and a look worth the price.

Same AI, different result. The difference came from the audit and design decisions, not from the tool: one step to a price instead of four, a clear reason to book direct, and a look that matches the place.

“The new site looks more premium and captures the feeling of a holiday by the ocean. I like that guests can now easily choose an apartment, check free dates, see the full price and message me directly.”

Anastasia, owner of Breeze
